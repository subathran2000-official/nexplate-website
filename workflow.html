HOW NEXPLATE WORKS

One guest journey.
Eight connected moments.

Your vision for a simple restaurant workflow, from the first welcome to the final payment.

01

Customer arrives

Guests sit at a table or place a takeaway order.

02

Waiter takes the order

A mobile or tablet app captures the order.

03

Order goes to POS

The order is recorded for the team.

04

Sent to the kitchen

A kitchen display or printer receives the ticket.

05

Kitchen prepares food

The kitchen prepares the meal and updates progress.

06

Food is served

The waiter delivers the meal to the guest.

07

Payment & receipt

The guest pays and receives a receipt.

08

Reports & table update

Sales, stock, and table information complete the service cycle.

NEXPLATE’s proposed workflow. Module availability and integrations will be confirmed during rollout.
