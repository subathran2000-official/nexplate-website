HOW NEXPLATE WORKS

One guest journey.
Eight moments of service.

Follow the journey from the first order to the final bill.

The eight steps of service

01

Customer arrives

Guests sit at a table or place a takeaway order.

02

Order is taken

A server takes the order at the table or the counter.

03

Order is recorded

The order is entered into the POS.

04

Kitchen is notified

The kitchen is told what to prepare.

05

Kitchen prepares food

The kitchen prepares the meal.

06

Food is served

The server delivers the meal to the guest.

07

Check is closed

The guest pays and the check is closed.

08

Table is reset

The table is cleared and ready for the next guests.
